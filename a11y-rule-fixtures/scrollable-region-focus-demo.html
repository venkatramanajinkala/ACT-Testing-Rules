ACT-R84 interactive demo: is the scrollable region keyboard reachable?

The region below always has real overflowing content in its inner element. Use the toggle to switch between the broken behavior and the fix, without reloading the page.

Broken (toggle off): the outer wrapper is purely visual and the inner content element carries tabindex="-1". Neither is ever in the Tab order — this is required, not optional: Chromium/Firefox automatically add any scrollable element with real overflow to the Tab order even with zero tabindex attributes anywhere, so tabindex="-1" is the documented way to opt back out.

Fixed (toggle on): the outer wrapper gets tabindex="0", role="region", and aria-label, plus a visible focus outline. While it's focused, arrow keys/PageUp/PageDown/Home/End scroll the inner element.

Test method: click somewhere else on the page first (e.g. this paragraph), then press Tab repeatedly and watch the Focus Inspector panel in the corner. Don't click directly into the scroll region first — clicking lets some browsers scroll content with arrow keys regardless of tabindex, which tests a different thing than Tab-reachability.

Unchecked = reproduces the bug (inner has tabindex="-1"). Checked = the corrected behavior.
